### Featured Engineering Projects

#### 🏥 Medicine Expiry Tracking & Transfer System (METTS)
![Flagship FYP](https://img.shields.io/badge/Type-Flagship_Final_Year_Project-8B5CF6?style=flat-square)
![Scale](https://img.shields.io/badge/Architecture-12_Modules_%C2%B7_60_Features-0A66C2?style=flat-square)
![Stack](https://img.shields.io/badge/Stack-Laravel_%C2%B7_PHP_%C2%B7_Blade_%C2%B7_Alpine.js_%C2%B7_MySQL_%C2%B7_Git-FF2D20?style=flat-square)

An enterprise healthcare inventory management platform engineered to eliminate pharmaceutical waste across multi-clinic networks through real-time batch tracking and automated inter-clinic stock redistribution.
- **12 Modules & 60 Features:** End-to-end architecture covering clinic inventory, batch lifecycles, user roles, and operational reporting.
- **FEFO (First-Expired, First-Out) Engine:** Automatically prioritizes and dispatches medicine batches closest to expiration to minimize wastage.
- **QR & Barcode Scanning:** Streamlines rapid stock intake, batch verification, and real-time audit logging for clinic staff.
- **Inter-Clinic Transfer Workflow:** Automates matching and redistribution requests between clinics holding surplus near-expiry stock and high-demand clinics.
- **Multilingual Dual-Channel Alerts:** Automated **SMS & Email** expiry notifications delivered in both **English and Roman Urdu**.
- **Role-Based Dashboards:** Administrative analytics tracking inventory lifecycles, transfer logs, and waste-reduction metrics.

#### 🏘️ Society Record System (WADZ)
![DSA Project](https://img.shields.io/badge/Type-Data_Structures_&_Algorithms-00599C?style=flat-square)
![Stack](https://img.shields.io/badge/Stack-C++_%C2%B7_OOP_%C2%B7_Doubly_Linked_Lists_%C2%B7_Stacks_%C2%B7_Recursion-004482?style=flat-square)

Console-based housing society management system engineered from scratch in C++ without external database dependencies.
- **Doubly Linked List (`Resident` Class):** Manages active resident records with dynamic CRUD operations (`add_resident()`, `update_number()`, `delete_resident()`, `display_record()`).
- **Linked-List Stack (`Stack` Class):** Maintains a LIFO archive (`push()`, `pop()`, `search_stack()`) for former residents who have vacated the society.
- **Search & Traversal:** Implements linear search algorithms, recursive 2D traversal reporting, and an interactive menu-driven CLI interface.

#### 🚗 Car Rental Management System
![System Design](https://img.shields.io/badge/Type-Multi--Module_Fleet_Platform-10B981?style=flat-square)
![Architecture](https://img.shields.io/badge/Roles-Admin_%C2%B7_Customer_%C2%B7_Dealer_%C2%B7_Accountant_%C2%B7_Invoice_Manager-059669?style=flat-square)

Centralized vehicle rental platform designed to digitize fleet bookings, renter verification, and automated financial billing across **5 operational roles**.
- **Security & Compliance:** Role-based authentication, driver's license verification, background checks, encrypted payment handling, and digital rental agreements.

- **Billing & Analytics:** Automated invoice generation, accounting reconciliation, and fleet utilization reporting charts.
